Freshly Baked Story

Our Whimsical Journey

From flour-covered aprons in a cozy home kitchen to spreading sweet smiles across the globe!

2013 — The Messy Beginning 👩‍🍳

Flour in Our Hair & Big Dreams

It all started in a tiny home kitchen with one oven, a broken whisk, and a whole lot of passion! Our first batch of cookies was slightly overbaked, but our neighbors loved them anyway (or maybe they were just being nice!). Natacakery was officially born!

2017 — Goodbye Garage! 🏬

Our Very First Boutique Shop

After filling our house with 500kg of flour bags, we realized we needed more space. We opened our tiny bakery shop in Jakarta! The smell of fresh croissants drew long queues every morning, and we almost ran out of butter every single weekend!

2021 — Spreading the Sweetness ✈️

Going Regional & Custom Madness

People started asking for custom birthday cakes, wedding tiers, and gift hampers. We expanded to Bandung & Surabaya, and launched our signature custom cake design service. Creating sweet memories became our daily superpower!

2026 — Worldwide Sweet Destinations 🌍

8 Global Branches & Counting!

Today, Natacakery serves happiness across 8 global destinations—from Tokyo and Seoul to New York and London! No matter where you are, our ovens are always baking warm treats made with 100% love and premium ingredients.
