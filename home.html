Philosophy of Taste

Flavor Notes

Vanilla
Vanilla represents familiar comfort — a gentle scent that instantly feels like home, no matter how simple life gets.
Chocolate
Chocolate is a warm hug on a long day — a touch of bitterness, deep sweetness, and ultimate satisfaction.
Matcha
Matcha brings mindful tranquility — subtle earthy notes that remind us to slow down and savor the present.
Red Velvet
Red Velvet embodies confident elegance — tender on the inside, boldly striking on the outside.

Taro is a pleasant surprise — unique in hue, soothing in taste, just like a lovely new discovery.
